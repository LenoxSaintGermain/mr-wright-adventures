# One-Page PRD: Mr. Wright Adventures Digital Platform

## Purpose

To build a minimal, trust-first digital platform that converts Mr. Wright's local relationships and expertise into a replicable, scalable, and premium concierge and logistics business for international travelers in Costa Rica.

## Success Criteria (90-Day Pilot)

*   Process 100+ trips through the platform.
*   Achieve a baggage loss/misroute rate of <3%.
*   Maintain a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of >8.
*   Demonstrate positive unit economics for VIP packages.

## Must-Have Features (MVP)

*   **Traveler Booking Flow:** Web-based booking with flight capture and service selection.
*   **Agent Job Management:** Simple interface (app or WhatsApp) for job assignment and proof of delivery.
*   **Baggage Tagging & Chain of Custody:** QR code-based system for secure luggage tracking.
*   **Notifications & ETA:** Automated updates for travelers.
*   **Payment Processing:** Secure online payments.

## Nice-to-Have Features (V1 and Beyond)

*   **Real-Time Map Tracking:** Live tracking of vehicles and agents.
*   **Subscription Concierge:** A premium subscription tier for enhanced services.
*   **SLA Dashboards:** Real-time monitoring of service quality.
*   **Partner Portal:** A dedicated portal for hotels, tour operators, and other partners.

## Key Risks & Mitigations

*   **Service Failure (Lost Luggage, No-Shows):** Mitigate with strict SLAs, backup agents, and clear communication.
*   **Regulatory/Insurance Exposure:** Mitigate with vetted partners, waivers, and clear service definitions.
*   **Overcommitment/Low Margins:** Mitigate by starting with high-margin services and scaling gradually.
