#### San José (SJO) Airport Hub

**Overview:**
Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) is Costa Rica's main gateway, located in Alajuela, 17 km northwest of San José. Most international flights arrive here, connecting you to the Central Pacific coast, Caribbean coast, and mountain destinations.

**Important to Know:**
- Heavy traffic around San José can add 30-60 minutes to drive times during peak hours (7-9 AM, 4-7 PM)
- Most beach destinations are 2-5 hours away
- Our drivers know alternate routes to avoid traffic delays
- All vehicles include Wi-Fi and refreshments for longer journeys

**Popular Destinations from SJO:**

| Destination | Distance | Drive Time | Best For |
|------------|----------|------------|----------|
| Manuel Antonio | 120 miles | 2-2.5 hours | Beaches, national park, family-friendly |
| Jacó Beach | 73 miles | 1-1.5 hours | Surfing, nightlife, quick beach access |
| La Fortuna (Arenal) | 94 miles | 3-3.5 hours | Volcano, hot springs, adventure activities |
| Tamarindo | 171 miles | 4.5-5 hours | Surfing, restaurants, beach town vibe |
| Monteverde | 105 miles | 3-3.5 hours | Cloud forest, nature, cooler climate |
| Puerto Viejo | 256 miles | 4.5-5 hours | Caribbean culture, laid-back beaches |

**Helicopter Alternative:**
For destinations over 2 hours away, consider our helicopter transfer service. Reduce a 4-hour drive to a 30-minute scenic flight. [Learn more about helicopter transfers]

#### Liberia (LIR) Airport Hub

**Overview:**
Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport (LIR) serves the Guanacaste region and northwest Pacific coast. This modern airport provides quick access to luxury resorts, surf towns, and pristine beaches.

**Important to Know:**
- Most destinations are 30 minutes to 1 hour away
- Roads are well-maintained in the main resort areas
- Dry season (December-April) offers the best road conditions
- Beach traffic can be heavy on weekends and holidays

**Popular Destinations from LIR:**

| Destination | Distance | Drive Time | Best For |
|------------|----------|------------|----------|
| Papagayo Peninsula | 28 miles | 45-50 minutes | Luxury resorts (Four Seasons, Andaz, Ritz-Carlton) |
| Tamarindo | 43 miles | 1 hour | Surfing, restaurants, nightlife |
| Playas del Coco | 38 miles | 40-45 minutes | Beach town, diving, family-friendly |
| Playa Flamingo | 53 miles | 55 minutes | Luxury villas, marina, sport fishing |
| Nosara | ~75 miles | 2-2.5 hours | Yoga, wellness, Blue Zone lifestyle |
| Santa Teresa | ~100 miles | 3+ hours | Bohemian surf town, remote beaches |

**Pro Tip:**
Flying into LIR gets you to the beach faster than SJO for all Guanacaste destinations. Perfect for maximizing beach time!

## FAQ Content

### Booking & Payment

**Q: When should I book my airport transfer?**
A: We recommend booking at least 48 hours before your arrival. During peak season (December-April), book 1-2 weeks in advance to guarantee vehicle availability.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment is processed securely through our platform.

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
A: Cancel up to 24 hours before service for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are subject to a 50% fee. No-shows are non-refundable.

**Q: Do I need to tip my driver?**
A: Tips are not included but are appreciated for excellent service. 10-15% is customary in Costa Rica.

### Logistics

**Q: What if my flight is delayed?**
A: We track all flights in real-time. If your flight is delayed, your driver adjusts automatically. No extra fees for delays.

**Q: What if I can't find my driver?**
A: Call or text the number provided in your confirmation. Your driver will guide you to the meeting point. We're also available 24/7 at [support number].

**Q: Can you accommodate child car seats?**
A: Yes! Specify the number and type of car seats needed when booking. We provide them at no extra charge.

**Q: What about extra luggage?**
A: Standard transfers include 2 bags per passenger. Extra luggage can be accommodated—just let us know when booking so we can assign the right vehicle.

### Service Areas

**Q: Do you serve all of Costa Rica?**
A: We serve all major tourist destinations from both SJO and LIR airports. If your destination isn't listed, contact us—we can likely accommodate.

**Q: Can you pick me up from my hotel for departure?**
A: Absolutely! We provide return transfers to the airport from any location we serve.

**Q: Do you operate during the rainy season?**
A: Yes, we operate year-round. During rainy season (May-November), we monitor road conditions and adjust routes as needed for safety.

## Seasonal Content

### High Season Travel Tips (December - April)

**What to Expect:**
- Perfect weather: sunny, dry, low humidity
- Higher prices across all services
- More crowds at popular beaches and attractions
- Advance booking essential for accommodations and services

**Our Recommendation:**
Book your airport transfer and key services 2-3 weeks in advance. Consider visiting less-crowded destinations like Uvita or the Caribbean coast for a more relaxed experience.

### Green Season Travel Tips (May - November)

**What to Expect:**
- Afternoon rain showers (mornings usually clear)
- Lush, vibrant landscapes
- Fewer tourists, more authentic experiences
- Lower prices on accommodations and services

**Our Recommendation:**
Plan outdoor activities for mornings. Pack a light rain jacket. Enjoy the lower prices and empty beaches—green season is underrated!
