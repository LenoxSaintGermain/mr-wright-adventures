# Mr. Wright Adventures - Partnership Framework

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** October 2025  
**Owner:** Mr. Wright / Operations Manager

---

## 1. Partnership Strategy

Our partnership strategy is built on a tiered system designed to cultivate deep, mutually beneficial relationships with a curated network of Costa Rica's premier service providers. The goal is to create a seamless, high-quality experience for our clients by integrating the best local services into our platform. 

**Core Principles:**
- **Quality over Quantity:** We partner with a select few providers in each category who meet our stringent quality standards.
- **Mutual Growth:** We aim to drive significant business to our partners and expect preferred service and pricing in return.
- **Seamless Integration:** Partnerships are managed through technology and clear communication to ensure a frictionless experience for the end customer.
- **Trust & Transparency:** All partnerships are governed by clear contracts, transparent commission structures, and regular performance reviews.

---

## 2. Partner Tiers & Benefits

### Tier 1: Strategic Partners

These are our most integrated partners, forming the backbone of our core service offerings. They receive the highest volume of referrals and are deeply embedded in our operational workflows.

**Who Qualifies:**
- Luxury Hotels & Resorts (e.g., Four Seasons, Andaz)
- High-volume Vacation Rental Management Companies
- Exclusive Helicopter Service Providers

**Benefits for Strategic Partners:**
- **Preferred Placement:** Top billing on our website and in client recommendations.
- **High-Volume Referrals:** Guaranteed monthly booking targets.
- **Co-Marketing:** Joint marketing campaigns, social media features, and email promotions.
- **Data Insights:** Shared analytics on customer trends and booking patterns.
- **Dedicated Account Manager:** A single point of contact at Mr. Wright Adventures.

**Our Expectations:**
- **Highest Commission/Best Rates:** Preferential pricing and the highest commission rates (15-20%).
- **Deep Integration:** API or direct booking system integration for real-time availability.
- **Exclusive Offers:** Special packages or perks available only to Mr. Wright clients.
- **24/7 Support:** A dedicated contact for urgent, after-hours requests.
- **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs):** Strategic meetings to align on goals and performance.

### Tier 2: Operational Partners

These partners provide the essential day-to-day services that support our core offerings. They are selected for their reliability, quality, and coverage.

**Who Qualifies:**
- Ground Transportation Fleets (SUVs, Vans)
- Top-rated Activity & Tour Operators (Zip-lining, Fishing, etc.)
- Vetted Medical Facilities (Hospitals, Clinics)
- Private Chefs & In-Villa Service Providers

**Benefits for Operational Partners:**
- **Consistent Bookings:** Regular stream of referrals based on client needs.
- **Simple Onboarding:** Easy integration into our booking system.
- **Prompt Payments:** Net-15 payment terms for all completed services.
- **Featured Listings:** Prominent placement within their respective service categories.

**Our Expectations:**
- **Competitive Commission:** Standard commission rates (10-15%).
- **Reliability & Professionalism:** Strict adherence to our SLAs for on-time service and quality.
- **Liability Insurance:** Proof of current and adequate insurance ($2M+).
- **Flexible Booking:** 24-hour cancellation policy and direct booking capabilities.
- **Monthly Reconciliations:** Simple, transparent invoicing and payment process.

### Tier 3: Service Partners

These partners provide ancillary services that add value to the client experience. The relationship is less formal but still managed for quality.

**Who Qualifies:**
- Top Restaurants & Bars
- Retailers & Personal Shoppers
- Specialty Service Providers (e.g., florists, decorators)

**Benefits for Service Partners:**
- **Targeted Referrals:** Recommendations to high-value clients.
- **No-Cost Marketing:** Inclusion in our 

curated lists and recommendations.
- **Priority Booking:** We handle the reservation process for our clients, simplifying their workflow.

**Our Expectations:**
- **Priority Reservations:** Ability to secure tables or appointments for our clients, even at short notice.
- **Exceptional Service:** Commitment to providing our clients with a consistently high-quality experience.
- **Direct Contact:** A dedicated person or contact line for handling our requests efficiently.

## 3. Partner Onboarding Process

Our onboarding process is designed to be thorough yet efficient, ensuring that every partner is fully aligned with our standards and integrated into our systems before receiving their first client.

| Step | Action | Description | Owner | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|----------|
| **1** | **Identification & Vetting** | We identify potential partners through market research, industry reputation, and direct referrals. A comprehensive vetting process includes online review analysis, a site visit (for Tier 1 & 2), and a secret shopper experience. | Operations Manager | 1-2 Weeks |
| **2** | **Initial Outreach** | A formal introduction is made, outlining the benefits of partnership and our expectations. We share our Partnership Framework document and schedule an introductory meeting. | Mr. Wright / Ops Mgr | 1 Week |
| **3** | **Negotiation & Contract** | We negotiate commission rates, service level agreements (SLAs), and other terms. A standardized contract is drafted, reviewed by both parties, and signed. | Mr. Wright | 1 Week |
| **4** | **System Integration** | The partner is added to our CRM and booking platform. We establish communication channels (e.g., dedicated WhatsApp contact) and integrate booking calendars or APIs where applicable (Tier 1). | Technology Lead | 1 Week |
| **5** | **Training & Activation** | We conduct a brief orientation on our brand standards, communication protocols, and the end-to-end client journey. The partner is then marked as 'Active' in our system and becomes eligible for bookings. | Operations Manager | 1-2 Days |

## 4. Partner Performance Management

We maintain a high-quality network by continuously monitoring partner performance. This ensures our clients always receive the best possible service and that our brand reputation is upheld.

| Metric | Target | Measurement Method | Review Frequency | Action for Underperformance |
|--------|--------|--------------------|------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Customer Feedback (NPS)** | Score > 8.5 | Post-service surveys sent to clients. | Monthly | 1. Joint review of feedback.<br>2. Develop a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).<br>3. If no improvement in 60 days, partnership is suspended. |
| **SLA Adherence** | 98% On-Time | GPS tracking data for transfers; timestamps for concierge responses. | Monthly | 1. Warning for first miss.<br>2. Financial penalty (e.g., reduced commission) for second miss.<br>3. Suspension for third miss. |
| **Booking Acceptance Rate** | >95% | Measured via the booking platform. | Quarterly | 1. Discuss capacity issues.<br>2. Adjust booking targets.<br>3. If consistently low, re-evaluate partnership tier. |
| **Communication Responsiveness** | <15 min (day), <60 min (night) | WhatsApp/email timestamps. | Monthly | 1. Gentle reminder of SLAs.<br>2. Escalate to partner management if recurring issue. |
| **Safety & Compliance** | 100% | Incident reports, annual insurance/permit checks. | Annually & On-Incident | **Immediate suspension** pending investigation for any safety violation. |

## 5. Partnership Agreement Template (Key Clauses)

All partnership agreements will be formalized in a written contract. Below are the key clauses that will be included to ensure clarity and protect both parties.

#### A. Services & Scope
- **Description of Services:** A detailed outline of the specific services the partner will provide (e.g., "VIP airport transfers in a luxury SUV").
- **Service Area:** The geographic regions covered by the partner.
- **Exclusivity:** Defines any exclusivity arrangements (if applicable).

#### B. Commission & Payment Terms
- **Commission Rate:** The specific percentage or flat fee per booking (e.g., "15% of the net booking value").
- **Invoicing Process:** The partner will submit a monthly invoice detailing all completed services with corresponding booking IDs.
- **Payment Terms:** Mr. Wright Adventures will pay all undisputed invoices within 15 days of receipt (Net-15).

#### C. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- **Quality Standards:** References the quality standards outlined in this framework (e.g., vehicle cleanliness, driver professionalism).
- **On-Time Performance:** Defines the window for on-time service (e.g., "drivers must be positioned 15 minutes prior to scheduled pickup").
- **Communication Protocols:** Mandates adherence to our communication response times.

#### D. Liability & Insurance
- **Insurance Requirement:** The partner must maintain a minimum of $2,000,000 in general liability insurance and provide a certificate of insurance naming Mr. Wright Adventures as an additional insured.
- **Indemnification:** Each party agrees to indemnify the other against claims arising from their own negligence.

#### E. Confidentiality
- **Client Information:** The partner agrees to keep all client information strictly confidential and use it only for the purpose of delivering the booked service.
- **Business Information:** Both parties agree to keep the terms of the partnership, including commission rates, confidential.

#### F. Term & Termination
- **Initial Term:** The standard contract term is one (1) year, with automatic renewal.
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate the agreement immediately for a material breach of contract (e.g., safety violation, repeated SLA failures).
- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate the agreement without cause by providing 30 days' written notice.
