## 1. Introduction to Journey Mapping

Customer journey maps are a visual representation of the end-to-end experience a customer has with Mr. Wright Adventures. They are critical for understanding our clients' needs, emotions, and pain points at every stage of their interaction with our brand. By mapping these journeys, we can identify moments of delight to amplify and friction points to eliminate, ensuring a consistently exceptional service.

These maps serve three primary purposes:
1.  **Empathy Building:** They help our team see the experience from the customer's perspective.
2.  **Process Improvement:** They highlight opportunities to streamline workflows and improve service delivery.
3.  **AI Training:** They provide a structured narrative of customer interactions that will form the knowledge base for our future AI assistants.
