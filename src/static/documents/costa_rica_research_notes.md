## Key Insights

1. **Long distances are the norm** - Most destinations are 2-5 hours from airports
2. **Two distinct regions** - SJO serves central/south/Caribbean, LIR serves northwest Pacific coast
3. **Traffic is a major factor** - Dense traffic in San José area adds significant time
4. **Road conditions vary** - Unpaved roads in rural areas, especially during rainy season
5. **Helicopter opportunity** - Long drives create strong value proposition for helicopter transfers

## Medical Tourism Market

Costa Rica is a **major medical tourism destination**, particularly for:
- **Plastic surgery** (facelifts, breast augmentation, liposuction, body contouring)
- **Dental procedures**
- **Orthopedic surgery**
- **Cosmetic procedures**

### Post-Op Recovery Facilities
- **Costa Rica Medical Center Inn** - Modern recovery inn designed for post-op care
- **Recovery Center Chetica** - Surgical aftercare retreat with 24/7 medical support
- Multiple clinics offer integrated surgery + recovery packages

### Key Insight for Mr. Wright
Medical tourists need:
- Airport pickup with privacy and comfort
- Luggage handling (may have mobility restrictions)
- Transportation to/from clinics and recovery facilities
- Concierge support for medications, groceries, follow-up appointments
- Discreet, professional service
- Post-op care coordination




## Tourism Statistics & Market Size

### Visitor Numbers
- **2024**: 2.66 million air arrivals (highest in 16 years, +7.7% vs 2023)
- **2019** (pre-pandemic peak): 3.14 million visitors
- **2024 H1**: 14.5% increase vs 2023 H1
- Costa Rica is the **most visited nation in Central America**
- Tourism is a **$1.7 billion industry**

### Visitor Demographics
- **54%** from United States
  - 21% from Florida
  - 16% from California
  - 8% from other states
- **18%** from Europe
  - France (2.8%)
  - Germany (2.6%)
  - United Kingdom (2.5%)
- **12%** from Latin America
- **5.6%** from Canada

### Seasonal Patterns

**HIGH SEASON (Dry Season): December - April**
- Peak months: December through mid-April
- Best weather: sunny, low humidity
- Spring break surge: mid to late April
- Highest prices and demand
- "Snowbirds" escaping North American winter

**GREEN SEASON (Rainy Season): May - November**
- Afternoon rain showers common
- Higher humidity
- Lower prices
- Fewer crowds
- Still viable for tourism (mornings often clear)

### Key Insight for Mr. Wright
- **Peak demand**: December-April (must scale operations)
- **Primary market**: US travelers (54%) - especially Florida and California
- **Secondary markets**: Europe (18%) and Canada (5.6%)
- **Long-haul travelers**: Need reliable airport transfers after international flights
- **Family travel**: Spring break (March-April) creates family travel surge
