# Costa Rica Market Research Summary

## The Two-Airport System

Costa Rica's tourism infrastructure is built around two international airports serving distinct geographic regions:

**Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO)**
- Location: Alajuela, 17 km northwest of San José
- Serves: Central Pacific coast, Caribbean coast, central mountain regions
- Largest and busiest airport
- Heavy traffic around San José adds complexity

**Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport (LIR)**
- Location: Liberia, Guanacaste
- Serves: Northwest Pacific coast, luxury resort corridor
- Modern facility, less traffic congestion
- Quick access to beach destinations

**Strategic Implication:** Mr. Wright needs dual-hub operations with agent networks in both regions.

## Tourism Statistics

### Visitor Volume
- **2.66 million** air arrivals in 2024 (+7.7% vs 2023)
- Most visited nation in Central America
- **$1.7 billion** tourism industry

### Demographics
- **54%** from United States (Florida 21%, California 16%)
- **18%** from Europe (France, Germany, UK)
- **5.6%** from Canada
- **12%** from Latin America

### Seasonal Patterns

**HIGH SEASON (December - April)**
- Dry season, perfect weather
- Peak demand, premium pricing
- Spring break surge (March-April)
- Must scale operations significantly

**GREEN SEASON (May - November)**
- Rainy season (afternoon showers)
- Lower prices, fewer crowds
- Mornings often clear and sunny
- Opportunity for testing and refinement

## Medical Tourism Market

Costa Rica is a **major medical tourism destination** for:
- Plastic surgery (facelifts, breast augmentation, liposuction)
- Dental procedures
- Orthopedic surgery
- Cosmetic procedures

### Specialized Facilities
- Costa Rica Medical Center Inn
- Recovery Center Chetica
- Multiple clinics with integrated surgery + recovery packages

### Service Needs
- Discreet, comfortable transportation
- Mobility-friendly vehicles
- Medication and appointment coordination
- Post-op concierge support
- 24/7 availability
- Less seasonal than general tourism

**Strategic Opportunity:** Underserved niche with high-value, year-round demand.

## Competitive Landscape

**Current State:**
- Fragmented, informal market
- Independent drivers and guides
- Word-of-mouth referrals
- Hotel concierge gatekeeping
- Limited tech integration

**Opportunity:**
- First-mover advantage for professional, platform-based service
- Uber operates in urban areas only, no specialized offerings
- No dominant player in premium segment
- Window to establish market leadership before larger competitors enter

## Strategic Imperatives

### Operational
1. **Dual-hub model** - Agent networks in both Guanacaste/Liberia and Central Pacific/San José regions
2. **Seasonal flexibility** - Scale up for high season (Dec-Apr), lean operations in green season
3. **Partnership focus** - Luxury resorts, property managers, medical facilities
4. **Service differentiation** - Go beyond basic transportation (luggage tracking, concierge, medical support)

### Marketing
1. **Target US travelers** - Primary market (54% of visitors)
2. **Emphasize reliability** - Long distances and traffic make professional service valuable
3. **Build trust signals** - Partnerships with Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, etc.
4. **Geographic messaging** - Different value props for SJO vs LIR arrivals

### Product
1. **Tiered pricing** - Economy, Comfort, VIP to address different segments
2. **Helicopter option** - Strong value prop for 4-5 hour drives
3. **Medical tourism packages** - Specialized post-op care bundles
4. **Vacation rental focus** - High-growth segment needing concierge support
