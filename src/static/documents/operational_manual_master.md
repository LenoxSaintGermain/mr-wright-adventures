## Service Delivery Workflows

### Workflow 1: VIP Airport Transfer (SJO/LIR)

#### Pre-Arrival (48-72 hours before)

**Step 1: Booking Confirmation**
- **Trigger:** Customer completes booking on platform OR agent enters booking manually
- **System Actions:**
  - Generate booking ID: `MWA-[YYYYMMDD]-[###]`
  - Send confirmation email/WhatsApp to customer
  - Create calendar event in Google Calendar
  - Assign to appropriate airport coordinator (SJO or LIR)
- **Human Actions:**
  - Airport coordinator reviews booking details
  - Confirms driver availability
  - Sends WhatsApp message to assigned driver

**Step 2: Flight Tracking Setup**
- **Trigger:** 48 hours before scheduled arrival
- **System Actions:**
  - Auto-populate flight details from booking
  - Set up flight tracking alerts (FlightAware, FlightRadar24)
- **Human Actions:**
  - Coordinator verifies flight number and arrival time
  - Updates booking if flight time changes
  - Sends reminder WhatsApp to driver

**Step 3: Customer Communication**
- **Trigger:** 24 hours before arrival
- **Message Template (WhatsApp):**
  ```
  Hi [CUSTOMER NAME]! 👋
  
  Your VIP airport transfer is confirmed for tomorrow.
  
  📅 Date: [DATE]
  ✈️ Flight: [AIRLINE] [FLIGHT#]
  🕐 Arrival: [TIME] at [SJO/LIR]
  🚗 Vehicle: [VEHICLE TYPE]
  👤 Driver: [DRIVER NAME]
  📍 Destination: [HOTEL/ADDRESS]
  
  Your driver will meet you at [MEETING POINT] with a sign showing your name.
  
  Driver contact: [DRIVER WHATSAPP]
  
  Need anything? Reply here anytime!
  
  - Mr. Wright Adventures Team
  ```
- **Human Actions:**
  - Coordinator personalizes message
  - Confirms customer received and read message

#### Day of Arrival

**Step 4: Driver Preparation**
- **Trigger:** 3 hours before flight arrival
- **Driver Checklist:**
  - [ ] Vehicle cleaned and fueled
  - [ ] Signage prepared with customer name
  - [ ] Route to destination reviewed (Waze/Google Maps)
  - [ ] Customer contact saved in phone
  - [ ] Bottled water and snacks loaded (VIP service)
  - [ ] Tablet charged for digital signature
  - [ ] Check-in with coordinator via WhatsApp

**Step 5: Airport Positioning**
- **Trigger:** Flight lands
- **Driver Actions:**
  - Position at designated meeting point (Arrivals Hall)
  - Hold signage with customer name
  - Monitor WhatsApp for customer messages
  - Coordinator monitors driver GPS location

**Step 6: Customer Pickup**
- **Trigger:** Customer makes contact
- **Driver Actions:**
  - Greet customer by name
  - Assist with luggage
  - Verify destination address
  - Offer water/snacks
  - Begin journey, share ETA via WhatsApp to coordinator
- **System Actions:**
  - GPS tracking active
  - Coordinator receives "Pickup Complete" notification

**Step 7: In-Transit**
- **Driver Responsibilities:**
  - Safe driving, follow speed limits
  - Provide local insights if customer is chatty
  - Monitor traffic, adjust route if needed
  - Send ETA update to customer if destination is >1 hour away
- **Coordinator Monitoring:**
  - Track GPS location
  - Available for driver questions/issues via WhatsApp

**Step 8: Drop-off & Completion**
- **Driver Actions:**
  - Assist with luggage to hotel/villa entrance
  - Confirm customer is checked in or greeted by property staff
  - Request digital signature on tablet (service confirmation)
  - Offer business card with 24/7 contact info
  - Send "Drop-off Complete" message to coordinator
- **System Actions:**
  - Update booking status to "Completed"
  - Trigger payment processing (if not prepaid)
  - Send post-service survey to customer (NPS)

**Step 9: Post-Service Follow-up**
- **Trigger:** 2 hours after drop-off
- **Message Template (WhatsApp):**
  ```
  Hi [CUSTOMER NAME],
  
  Hope you're settling in! 🌴
  
  How was your transfer with [DRIVER NAME]?
  
  We'd love your feedback: [SURVEY LINK]
  
  Need anything during your stay? Restaurant reservations, tours, or transportation?
  
  Just reply here - we're available 24/7!
  
  Pura Vida! 🇨🇷
  - Mr. Wright Adventures
  ```

#### Exception Handling

**Flight Delay**
- **Trigger:** Flight tracking shows >30 min delay
- **Actions:**
  - Coordinator notifies driver
  - Driver adjusts schedule
  - Customer receives update via WhatsApp
  - No additional charge for wait time <2 hours

**Flight Cancellation**
- **Trigger:** Flight tracking shows cancellation
- **Actions:**
  - Coordinator contacts customer immediately
  - Offer to reschedule or refund
  - Cancel driver assignment
  - Update booking status

**Customer No-Show**
- **Trigger:** 30 minutes after flight arrival, no customer contact
- **Actions:**
  - Driver attempts WhatsApp/call (3 attempts, 10 min apart)
  - Coordinator attempts contact
  - After 1 hour: Service marked as no-show
  - No-show fee applies per policy (50% of booking)

**Vehicle Breakdown**
- **Trigger:** Driver reports issue
- **Actions:**
  - Coordinator dispatches backup vehicle immediately
  - Customer notified of slight delay
  - Driver stays with customer until backup arrives
  - Incident report filed for maintenance

### Workflow 2: Helicopter Transfer

#### Pre-Flight (72 hours before)

**Step 1: Booking & Feasibility Check**
- **Trigger:** Customer requests helicopter transfer
- **Coordinator Actions:**
  - Verify route is within helicopter range
  - Check weather forecast for departure date
  - Confirm landing zone availability at destination
  - Get quote from helicopter partner
  - Send quote to customer with 48-hour hold

**Step 2: Booking Confirmation**
- **Trigger:** Customer accepts quote and pays deposit (50%)
- **System Actions:**
  - Generate booking ID: `MWA-HELI-[YYYYMMDD]-[###]`
  - Create calendar event
  - Send confirmation to customer
- **Coordinator Actions:**
  - Confirm booking with helicopter partner
  - Collect passenger details (names, weights, passport info)
  - Send safety briefing document to customer

**Step 3: Pre-Flight Coordination**
- **Trigger:** 48 hours before flight
- **Coordinator Actions:**
  - Reconfirm weather conditions
  - Verify landing zone clearance
  - Arrange ground transportation to/from helipads
  - Send detailed itinerary to customer

#### Day of Flight

**Step 4: Final Checks**
- **Trigger:** 3 hours before departure
- **Pilot Actions:**
  - Weather check (wind, visibility, ceiling)
  - Aircraft pre-flight inspection
  - Flight plan filed
  - Fuel check
- **Coordinator Actions:**
  - Confirm customer is en route to departure point
  - Notify destination property of arrival time

**Step 5: Passenger Briefing**
- **Trigger:** Customer arrives at helipad
- **Pilot Actions:**
  - Safety briefing (headsets, seatbelts, emergency procedures)
  - Weight and balance check
  - Secure luggage
  - Passenger boarding

**Step 6: Flight**
- **Pilot Responsibilities:**
  - Safe flight operations
  - Scenic route when possible
  - Point out landmarks
  - Smooth landing
- **Coordinator Monitoring:**
  - Track flight progress
  - Notify destination of ETA

**Step 7: Arrival & Handoff**
- **Pilot Actions:**
  - Assist passengers with disembarkation
  - Unload luggage
  - Handoff to ground transportation or property staff
- **Coordinator Actions:**
  - Confirm safe arrival
  - Process final payment
  - Send thank-you message

### Workflow 3: Concierge Service Request

#### Request Intake

**Step 1: Customer Request**
- **Channels:** WhatsApp, email, platform message
- **Concierge Actions:**
  - Acknowledge receipt within 15 minutes (24/7)
  - Clarify request details (date, time, preferences, budget)
  - Assign to specialist if needed (dining, activities, medical)

**Step 2: Research & Options**
- **Concierge Actions:**
  - Research available options
  - Contact partners for availability and pricing
  - Prepare 2-3 options with pros/cons
  - Send recommendations to customer within 2 hours

**Step 3: Booking & Confirmation**
- **Trigger:** Customer selects option
- **Concierge Actions:**
  - Confirm booking with partner
  - Process payment (if applicable)
  - Send confirmation details to customer
  - Add to customer itinerary
  - Set reminder for follow-up

#### Service Delivery

**Step 4: Pre-Service Reminder**
- **Trigger:** 24 hours before service
- **Message Template:**
  ```
  Hi [NAME],
  
  Reminder: Your [SERVICE] is confirmed for tomorrow!
  
  📅 [DATE] at [TIME]
  📍 [LOCATION]
  👤 Contact: [PROVIDER NAME] - [PHONE]
  
  [SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS]

  ```

**Step 5: Day-of Coordination**
- **Concierge Actions:**
  - Confirm with provider that service is ready
  - Check in with customer 1 hour before
  - Be available for any issues

**Step 6: Post-Service Follow-up**
- **Trigger:** After service completion
- **Concierge Actions:**
  - Ask for feedback
  - Address any issues immediately
  - Log feedback in CRM
  - Send thank-you message
